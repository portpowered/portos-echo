| 2026-11-16 | **Credentials may live on the device, and the bridge's role moves onto the Echo** (owner: "its fine to keep the credential there, we should validate how openclaw does it, and replicate, i believe its fine to keep credentials there generally"). This supersedes "No credential reaches the Echo" (plan section 3) as the target; until the move, the credentials stay on the bridge host as today. What OpenClaw does (read at `032227a`): one process, its gateway, holds the ChatGPT sign-in in its auth-profile store and refreshes it there. A client that wants a Live call (the iOS, Android and watch apps, the browser) never receives the credential. It gets a one-time offer token that lasts 60 s and an offer URL on the gateway, `/plugins/openai/realtime/calls`. It POSTs its SDP offer there, and the gateway forwards it to `chatgpt.com/backend-api/codex/realtime/calls` with the OAuth token and returns the answer (`extensions/openai/realtime-quicksilver-session.ts` L54-L56 and L234-L257, `apps/ios/Sources/Voice/TalkRealtimeWebRTCSession.swift` L1117-L1129). Media then flows from the client straight to OpenAI, while the token and the control channel (tools, delegation) stay with the gateway. With a Platform key the gateway mints an ephemeral client secret instead (`realtime-voice-provider-factory.ts` L429-L459). What we replicate: on the Echo the gateway is our bridge process, so the credential is on the device, but only that process can read it: its own user, a file of mode 0600 that neither hassmic nor the screen app can read. Anything else on the device gets a one-time token, never the credential, the same rule as OpenClaw's clients. This closes the open question "What replaces Wyoming between the bridge and the Echo?" with (c): the bridge runs on the Echo and talks to GPT-Live directly. Wyoming shrinks to a loopback link to hassmic behind `echo-link`, until the fork gives hassmic a generic local link. It also closes "Where does the bridge run?": the Echo is the target, and the PC stays the development and bench host. Order: M1 passes on the PC bridge first, since it proves the bridge itself and is close.
