| 2026-11-18 | **Reply audio gets a NetEQ-style adaptive playout cushion, in the bridge.** Owner: "as an extension of our bridge/hassmic wiring for the continuation buffer that we should consider netEQ or some type of natural buffering to fix some of the buffering problems/late arrivals". Measured with the fixed 100 ms prime: one underrun of about 100 ms right after a 279 ms arrival gap (`baselines.md`); the prime absorbs ordinary jitter, not a stall, and a dry device cuts off mid-wave. Built in the bridge alone; hassmic and `echo-contracts` are unchanged. (1) Target: each accepted frame's lateness is measured against the 20 ms audio clock, from the earliest frame of the last one to two seconds. The worst lateness counts in full for 20 s (the measured stalls came about every 9 s of reply) and is then forgotten at 5 ms a second. (2) Underrun: an unfinished reply that stops in mid-speech gets one fade-out frame (its last frame mirrored and ramped to silence) when the modelled device has 20 ms left, the restart waits for the target, and the first frame after fades in.
